# BenchWeave Discord forum posting guidelines

Use these guidelines when starting a thread in a BenchWeave forum. A clear post helps others understand the topic and give a useful answer.

1. **Choose the right forum and tags.** Check for an existing thread on the same topic before starting a new one. Add the tags that best describe your post.
2. **Write a specific title.** Say what you need or what you found. For example, “Gateway cannot discover a USB device on Linux” is more useful than “Help needed”.
3. **Give enough context.** Explain what you are trying to do, what happened, what you expected, and what you have already tried. For technical questions, include relevant BenchWeave version, operating system, device or integration, and the exact error text where available.
4. **Make evidence easy to read.** Put commands, logs and error messages in code blocks. Share screenshots or links when they help, and explain what they show.
5. **Protect private information.** Remove passwords, tokens, personal details, private URLs and sensitive data from logs and screenshots before posting.
6. **Keep discussion constructive.** Be respectful, stay on topic, and critique ideas or behaviour rather than people. Avoid duplicate posts and repeated pings for attention.
7. **Close the loop.** Reply with the solution or outcome when you find it. If your situation changes, edit the opening post so later readers can follow the thread.

## Example post

**Title:** Gateway cannot discover a USB device on Linux

**Tags:** Choose the relevant tags available in the forum.

> I am trying to connect a USB device to a BenchWeave gateway on Linux. I expected it to appear during discovery, but no device is listed. I have checked the cable and restarted the gateway. BenchWeave version: [version]. Operating system: [distribution and version]. Device: [model]. Relevant log output: [sanitised code block]. What should I check next?
